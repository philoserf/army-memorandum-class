MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

SUBJECT: A memorandum containing quoted text


1. The regulation calls this “correspondence” and the class turns the typed ASCII quotes around it
into a typographic pair.

2. Nesting does NOT work, and this item is the record of that. One active character cannot tell
an inner quote from an outer one, so csquotes simply alternates: the reviewer wrote “the term
”correspondence“ is defined in paragraph 1-1” here, and the inner pair comes out closing-then-
opening. Use \enquote for anything nested: “the term ‘correspondence’ is defined in paragraph
1-1”.

3. To get a literal typewriter quote instead, write \textquotedbl, which renders as " and is
unaffected by csquotes.
