% the paragraph whole and never exercise the rule. See #124.
%

% is deliberate: the golden has to survive a TeX Live font-metric change. Prose
% filler reflows when metrics move and takes the page break with it -- which is
% why examples/example-pagebottom.tex calls itself the most fragile golden here.
